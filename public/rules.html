Server Regulations

Ignorance of the rules is not an excuse. Read carefully to maintain your standing.

1

No Cheating / Clients

The use of any unauthorized client modifications, hacks, x-ray texture packs, or auto-clickers is strictly prohibited. Our anti-cheat will permanently ban violators.

2

Respect All Players

Harassment, hate speech, racism, and extreme toxicity are not tolerated. Trash talk during PvP is fine, but crossing the line into personal attacks will result in a mute or ban.

3

No Bug Abuse

Exploiting server bugs, duplication glitches, or abusing mechanics intended to give an unfair advantage will result in an immediate economy wipe and ban.

4

No Advertising

Promoting other Minecraft servers, Discord communities, or unauthorized websites in the public chat or via private messages is highly restricted.
