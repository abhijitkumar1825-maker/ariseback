⚔️ ARISE SMP – Server Rules

Welcome to ARISE SMP! 🌌 To ensure a fair and enjoyable experience, every player must follow these rules.

📜 General Rules

  • 🛡️ Respect Everyone – No harassment, hate speech, or discrimination.
  • 💬 Keep Chat Clean – No excessive spam or inappropriate content.
  • 🎭 No Impersonation – Do not impersonate staff or other players.

⚔️ Gameplay Rules

  • 🚫 No Hacks or Cheats – Hacked clients, X-Ray, auto-clickers, macros, scripts, exploits, or any unfair advantage are strictly prohibited.
  • 🎮 No Bug Abuse – Exploiting glitches, dupes, or unintended mechanics is not allowed.
  • 🏕️ No Spawn Griefing – Do not grief or trap protected spawn areas.

🏠 Building Rules

  • 🏗️ Respect Player Builds unless server mechanics explicitly allow otherwise.
  • 🔥 No Lag Machines or builds designed to intentionally reduce server performance.

💰 Economy & Scamming Policy

  • 💸 Scamming is ALLOWED at your own risk.
  • ⚠️ ARISE SMP Staff will NOT refund items, money, or valuables lost through scams, trades, giveaways, or player-to-player deals.
  • 🤝 Trade carefully. Double-check every deal before accepting. Once a trade is completed, it is considered final.
  • 🚫 Real-money scams, account theft, phishing, or attempts to steal accounts are strictly forbidden and will result in a permanent ban.

👥 Community & Staff Decisions

  • 📢 No advertising other servers or Discords without permission.
  • 🎉 Help keep the community friendly and enjoyable.
  • ⚖️ Respect staff decisions. If you believe a punishment was unfair, open a support ticket instead of arguing in chat.

🚨 Punishments

Rule violations may result in:

  • • 🟡 Warning
  • • 🟠 Temporary Mute
  • • 🔴 Temporary Ban
  • • ⛔ Permanent Ban
✨ Trade wisely. Play fairly. Rise stronger. Welcome to ARISE SMP!
