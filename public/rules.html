Server Regulations

Welcome to Arise SMP! To keep our server fun, fair, and safe for everyone, all players must adhere to the following rules.

🛡️

Fair Play & Server Integrity

  • No Hacking or Cheating: The use of hacked clients, X-Ray texture packs, auto-clickers, or exploit mods is strictly prohibited.
  • No Exploiting Bugs: If you discover a glitch or duping exploit, report it immediately to the staff team. Exploiting bugs will result in an immediate ban.
  • Lag Machines: Do not build intentional lag machines or redstone loops that impair server performance.
💬

Chat & Community Etiquette

  • Be Respectful: Treat all members and staff with courtesy. Toxicity, harassment, or personal attacks will not be tolerated.
  • Zero Tolerance Policy: Hate speech, discrimination, offensive slurs, or inappropriate content are strictly forbidden.
  • No Spamming: Keep chat readable. Avoid excessive caps, spamming messages, or advertising other servers/websites.
⚔️

Gameplay & PvP

  • PvP Consent: PvP is only allowed when both players agree, unless designated in specific open-PvP zones or lore events.
  • Griefing & Stealing: Respect other players' builds and property. Griefing or stealing from claimed areas/unclaimed bases without permission is not allowed.
  • Spawn Protection: Spawn is a safe zone. No PvP, traps, or lava placement around spawn areas.
⚖️

Enforcement & Punishment

  • Moderator Discretion: Server administrators and moderators reserve the right to warn, kick, or ban players who break these rules. Punishments may vary based on the severity and frequency of the infraction.
  • Support Tickets: If you notice someone breaking the rules, please open a Support Ticket in our Discord server with evidence (screenshots/video). Have fun and happy building!
